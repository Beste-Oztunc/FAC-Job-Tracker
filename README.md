# FAC-Job-Tracker
FAC started as a project to keep me busy and hopefully build something usefu —after I was laid off.
FAC (Fully Automated Candidate) FAC collects public job postings, removes noise, scores opportunities using rules, and uses AI only when it is genuinely helpful as market insights or career coach. The goal is to spend less time digging through job-board landfill and more time reviewing roles that may actually be worth applying to.
FAC currently supports Greenhouse, Ashby, and Lever. I would eventually like to add Workday, iCIMS, and other platforms, although those systems are more difficult to access consistently through public endpoints. A likely approach will be targeted support for specific companies that users care about rather than attempting to crawl every employer on those platforms.
LinkedIn scraping is not currently a priority. There is already an enormous amount of duplication there: jobs are repeatedly reposted, expired opportunities remain visible, and the same position may appear as dozens of separate listings simply because multiple locations were attached to it. FAC is meant to reduce that noise, not reproduce it.
The project is local-first. Deterministic filtering and scoring remain separate from optional AI analysis, and FAC does not include telemetry, advertising, or analytics.
Frontend development is not my strongest area, so contributions are especially welcome there—as well as for accessibility, documentation, ATS integrations, testing, and general usability.
FAC is free for individual job seekers under the FAC Job Seeker Community License. It can be used, modified, forked, self-hosted, and shared by people looking for work. The restrictions are intended to prevent corporate, recruiting, HR, and commercial exploitation.
Good luck with your job search. I hope FAC helps make it a little less miserable.
