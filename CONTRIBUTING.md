# Contributing to FAC - Job Tracker

FAC exists to help individual job seekers. Contributions that make it more
useful, understandable, accessible, private, or reliable are welcome.

Small fixes may be submitted directly. For a large feature or architectural
change, opening an issue first will help avoid duplicated work.

Please do not submit proprietary code, private employer information,
candidate data, resumes, API keys, or anything copied from another project
without permission.

By contributing, you confirm that you have the right to submit the work and
agree that your contribution may be included in FAC under the FAC Job
Seeker Community License.

FAC should remain local-first and respectful of job seekers. In practice,
that means keeping API keys out of browser code, avoiding tracking and
advertising, keeping AI optional, and preserving Mac and Windows support
where practical.

Frontend contributions, accessibility improvements, and cross-platform
installer testing are especially welcome.
