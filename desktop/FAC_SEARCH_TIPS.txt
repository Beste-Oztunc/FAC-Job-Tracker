FAC - JOB TRACKER
RESUME PREPARATION AND SEARCH TIPS
==================================

FAC works best when it receives clear, truthful information about your
experience and the kinds of jobs you actually want. You do not need to
rewrite your entire resume specifically for FAC, but a few small choices
can improve both the search results and the optional AI analysis.


1. PREPARING YOUR RESUME TEXT
-----------------------------

Paste the full text of your most complete resume into the Resume Text box.

Plain text is perfect. FAC does not need the original PDF or Word
formatting.

Include:

- your current and previous job titles;
- a short professional summary, if you have one;
- responsibilities that show what you actually did;
- accomplishments and measurable results;
- industries and business areas you understand;
- tools, platforms, programming languages, and technical methods;
- certifications;
- education;
- customer-facing, leadership, research, or project experience that may
  matter for your target roles.

Strong resume content is specific. For example:

    Built credit-risk models using Python, SQL, and logistic regression.

is more useful than:

    Worked on analytical projects.

And:

    Led technical discovery, demonstrations, and proof-of-concept design
    for enterprise banking customers.

is more useful than:

    Supported sales.

Use numbers when they are truthful and meaningful, such as:

- revenue or deal size;
- percentage improvement;
- number of clients, users, models, or projects;
- time saved;
- portfolio size;
- recovery, conversion, fraud-loss, or performance improvement.

Do not add a skill only because it appears in job descriptions. FAC can
help identify gaps, but the resume should remain an accurate record of
your experience.

You may use your longest or "master" resume. It is okay if it contains more
detail than the shorter version you normally submit with applications.


2. WHAT TO PUT IN "DESIRED JOBS"
--------------------------------

Use this box to describe the roles and career directions you genuinely
want FAC to prioritize.

You can enter job titles separated by commas or put each one on a new line.

Example:

    Solutions Engineer
    Solutions Consultant
    Sales Engineer
    Technical Consultant
    Customer Engineer

You can also include closely related specializations:

    AI Solutions Engineer
    Fraud Solutions Consultant
    Credit Risk Data Scientist
    Model Risk Consultant
    Machine Learning Engineer

Use titles that employers actually use. A quick way to build the list is
to look at several jobs you would happily apply to and copy the recurring
job-title language.

Include reasonable title variations. Employers may describe nearly the
same job as:

    Solutions Engineer
    Solution Engineer
    Sales Engineer
    Presales Engineer
    Pre-Sales Consultant
    Solutions Consultant

Do not put every remotely possible job into this box. A focused list
usually produces clearer results than a list containing unrelated career
paths.

A good starting range is approximately 5 to 15 related titles.


3. DESCRIBING MORE THAN ONE CAREER DIRECTION
--------------------------------------------

FAC can support more than one direction, but group related searches
together where possible.

Good combination:

    Solutions Engineer
    Solutions Consultant
    Technical Consultant
    Customer Engineer
    Solutions Architect

These roles share similar responsibilities.

Broader combination:

    Data Scientist
    Economist
    Solutions Engineer
    Product Manager
    Technical Writer

This may still be useful, but the scoring rules will need more careful
adjustment because the roles are not closely related.

When two career directions are very different, consider saving separate
configurations or running FAC twice with different target terms.


4. LOCATION PREFERENCES
-----------------------

Be specific about what is acceptable.

Example:

    Remote anywhere in the United States. Open to hybrid work within the
    Raleigh-Durham area. Open to relocation for an exceptional role.

Other useful details may include:

- cities or states you prefer;
- whether you are open to relocation;
- whether occasional travel is acceptable;
- whether fully onsite work is acceptable;
- whether "remote" must mean remote from your current state;
- countries or regions where you are authorized to work.

Location preferences guide the optional AI coach. FAC's deterministic
location handling is controlled separately by the Local-area, Remote,
Hybrid, and Onsite terms in the scoring settings.


5. USING TARGET ROLE PHRASES
----------------------------

Target Role Phrases are the strongest title signals in FAC.

Use complete job titles or very common title phrases, such as:

    solutions engineer
    solution consultant
    sales engineer
    data scientist
    economist

Give the highest points to the roles you most want.

Do not add broad words such as:

    manager
    analyst
    engineer
    consultant

by themselves unless you truly want nearly every title containing that
word. Broad terms can create noisy results.


6. USING TITLE SPECIALTY TERMS
------------------------------

Title Specialty Terms reward important areas that appear directly in the
job title.

Examples:

    fraud
    credit risk
    model risk
    artificial intelligence
    machine learning
    financial services
    decisioning

These terms help distinguish:

    Solutions Engineer

from:

    Fraud Solutions Engineer

or:

    AI Solutions Engineer

Use specialties that materially increase your interest in a role.


7. USING DOMAIN TERMS
---------------------

Domain Terms look for relevant concepts in the job description.

Examples:

    banking
    lending
    underwriting
    fraud
    insurance
    machine learning
    model governance
    generative AI

Choose terms that reflect experience you already have or areas you
seriously want to pursue.

Avoid adding dozens of very general business words. A smaller set of
meaningful domain terms usually works better.


8. USING JOB TERMS
------------------

Job Terms are additional phrases in the description that make a role more
attractive or more aligned with how you like to work.

Examples:

    proof of concept
    discovery
    demonstration
    solution design
    technical sales
    workshop
    API integration
    customer-facing
    executive presentation

These do not have to be job titles. They describe responsibilities,
activities, or work styles you want to see.


9. USING EXCLUDED JOB TERMS
---------------------------

Use Excluded Job Terms for jobs you clearly do not want.

Examples:

    intern
    internship
    new graduate
    entry level
    junior
    commission only

Be careful with very broad exclusions.

For example, excluding:

    associate

may remove both entry-level roles and legitimate senior titles such as:

    Senior Associate Director

Start with obvious exclusions, review the results, and add more only when
you see repeated unwanted patterns.


10. CHOOSING COMPANIES
----------------------

The core company list is a good starting point because it keeps collection
fast.

Enable more companies when:

- your target industry is underrepresented;
- you are willing to consider smaller or less familiar employers;
- you want broader market intelligence;
- you know a company uses Greenhouse, Ashby, or Lever.

Adding every company creates a broader search but can make board discovery
slower. More companies do not automatically mean better matches.


11. SCORE THRESHOLDS
--------------------

The default thresholds are a reasonable starting point:

    Possible match: 50
    Strong match:   65

After your first run:

- lower the possible-match threshold slightly if too few plausible jobs
  appear;
- raise it if the dashboard contains too much noise;
- change term weights before making large threshold changes;
- inspect why high-scoring jobs earned their points.

The score is a prioritization tool, not a verdict. A lower-scoring job may
still be excellent, and a high-scoring job may contain a requirement that
makes it unsuitable.


12. OPTIONAL AI ANALYSIS
------------------------

AI analysis is optional.

When enabled, FAC may use your resume text, preferences, and selected job
information to generate:

- market interpretation;
- likely strengths;
- possible gaps;
- certification priorities;
- interview preparation;
- recommendations for the most relevant resume version.

AI analysis does not replace the deterministic score. It is a separate
interpretation layer and may make mistakes.

Review all generated guidance before changing a resume, preparing an
application, or making a career decision.


13. A SIMPLE FIRST-RUN RECIPE
-----------------------------

For a useful first run:

1. Paste your complete, truthful master resume.
2. Add 5 to 15 related desired job titles.
3. State your location and work-arrangement preferences clearly.
4. Keep the default core company list.
5. Review the default role, specialty, domain, and job terms.
6. Remove terms that do not fit you.
7. Add a few highly relevant terms rather than dozens of broad keywords.
8. Keep AI off for the first run if you only want to test collection and
   scoring.
9. Review the dashboard.
10. Adjust weights and exclusions based on the jobs you actually see.

FAC improves through iteration. The first configuration does not need to
be perfect.
